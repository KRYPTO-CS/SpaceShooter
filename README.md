# 🚀 SpaceShooter

## 🪐 Overview
**SpaceShooter** is a fast-paced arcade-style game built in **Godot 4** for the **TaskBlast** application! Players pilot a ship through waves of asteroids and collect power-ups to enhance their capabilities. The goal is simple: dodge and destroy asteroids, rack up points, and upgrade your firepower with a variety of unique blaster and shooting types.

---

## 🎮 Controls
Tap the screen and drag, and your ship will follow your motion. That's all there is to it!

---

## ⚙️ Mechanics
- **Dynamic Difficulty:** Speed and asteroid rates scale as the game progresses!
- **Score:** Earn points by destroying asteroids! Don't get hit though, or else you'll lose points...
- **Powerups:** Collect powerups to enhance your asteroid-blasting power!

---

## 🔋 Powerups
Powerups appear randomly and enhance your ship’s capablities when collected. With 3 blaster types and 7 shooting types, there are a total of 21 combinations!

### 💥 Blaster Types
Blaster types define what kind of **projectile** your ship fires. 

| Blaster Type | Description |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Simple** | Standard energy blasts with balanced speed and power. |
| **Drill** | Pierces through multiple asteroids dealing heavy damage, but moves and fires slower. |
| **Swift** | Does low damage, but will fire faster and faster as you hit asteroids. |

### 🔫 Shooting Types
Shooting types determine **how** your weapon fires.

| Shooting Type | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Normal** | Fires a single blast per shot at a medium rate. |
| **Charge** | Hold to charge up, release to shoot a powerful blast. |
| **Triple** | Fires three blasts in a spread pattern, but at a slower rate. |
| **Burst** | Rapidly fires a burst of five weak blasts. |
| **Spray** | Fires a fast stream of inaccurate blasts. |
| **V** | Shoots two alternating blasts diagonally in a V formation at a fast rate. |
| **Delta** | Fires three blasts in a triangular pattern at a slower rate. |
